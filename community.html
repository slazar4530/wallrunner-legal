Wall Runner community rules

Version 1 · Effective 29 October 2026

These rules apply to player names, published Trap Duel rooms and interactions with other players. You must accept them in the game before publishing a room.

  • Keep names and rooms respectful. No hate, sexual content, threats, harassment or targeting other players.
  • Do not publish personal information or content you do not have rights to share.
  • Do not use room layouts or names to communicate prohibited content.
  • Use reporting honestly. Do not abuse reports to target a rival.

Reporting and blocking

Use REPORT beside a room in the browser to choose a reason or block its builder. Manage blocked players in Settings > Help, privacy & safety. Blocking hides their rooms and prevents new pairings between you. Existing completed duel results remain recorded.

Moderation

The developer reviews reports and may hide a builder's rooms, restrict publishing, or take other proportionate action. Reports do not automatically remove content. Reports are private.

For urgent concerns, appeals or help, email slazar4530@gmail.com and include your player ID. See the privacy policy for data handling and deletion.
