Wall Runner - Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

This policy explains what the mobile game Wall Runner ("the game", "we") collects, why, who handles it, and how you can have it deleted. It applies to the game on Android, distributed through Google Play.

What the game collects

  • A player ID. When the game goes online it signs you in to Unity Gaming Services without a name or an account, and gets a player ID of the game. If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games (when the game offers it), your Google Play Games account is linked to that player ID; the game does not read your email address, your contacts or your Google account's other data.
  • Your game progress. The levels you finished, your best times, the coins you collected, your achievements, your settings and the looks you chose are kept on the phone. If you sign in with Google Play Games, a copy is kept in the cloud, so that your progress is not lost when you change or reset your phone.
  • Your name and your runner. When you start the game you choose a name (3 to 12 English letters and digits; a random one is offered) and a runner. Both are kept in your progress and shown to other players on the leaderboards. Choose a name that does not identify you if you prefer.
  • Your scores on the leaderboards. Your best time on each level and the coins you collected are sent to the game's leaderboards with your player ID, your name (the one you chose; until you choose one, a name the service makes up, or your Google Play Games name if you signed in) and the runner you ran as. Other players can see that name, the runner, your times, your coins and your place.
  • Your purchases. When you buy something in the game, Google Play takes the payment. The game receives and keeps a record of what you bought, so that you get it and keep it; it never sees your payment details.
  • Ads. The game shows ads from third-party advertising networks. To show and measure ads, these networks collect your device's advertising ID, information about your device, an approximate location worked out from your IP address, and how you interact with the ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised for you, and you can change your choice. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

The game does not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, files, payment details or your Google account's email address.

Why it is collected

  • To sign you in and find your progress again (account management).
  • To save your progress and bring it back on another phone you sign in on (app functionality).
  • To run the leaderboards (app functionality).
  • To deliver what you buy and keep a record of it (app functionality).
  • To show ads and measure them (advertising, by the advertising networks).

We do not sell your data.

Who handles it

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services: Authentication, Cloud Save and Leaderboards) stores your player ID, your cloud copy and your scores on our behalf. See Unity's privacy policy.
  • Google (Google Play Games Services) signs you in if you choose to. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Play (Google Play Billing) takes the payments for purchases.
  • Advertising networks show the ads and collect what is described above under their own privacy policies: Unity LevelPlay (Unity's privacy policy for players) and Google AdMob (how Google uses information from apps that use its services), and the networks LevelPlay works with.

Apart from what the advertising networks collect to show ads, we do not share your data with anyone else. Everything sent between the game and these services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

How long it is kept

Your player ID, cloud copy, scores and purchase records are kept for as long as your player exists. The copy on your phone is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Deleting your data

You can ask for your player and all data kept for it to be deleted at any time. Send an email to slazar4530@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Wall Runner data" and the name the leaderboards show for you. We delete your player ID, your name, your cloud copy and your entries on the leaderboards within 30 days, and answer you when it is done. A record of purchases may be kept as long as the law requires. Uninstalling the game alone does not delete what is kept in the cloud.

Replays, shared rooms and community safety

Competitive runs include a record of gameplay taps, checkpoint returns and fast-forward timing. Our server replays this record to verify results. A verified run may be shown to other players as a racing ghost.

Trap Duel lets you publish rooms under your chosen player name and runner appearance. Other players can browse and play those rooms. Do not include personal information in your name or room content.

When you report content, we store your player ID, the reported player and room, the selected reason, the date and review status for moderation. We also keep your blocked-player list and acceptance of the community rules. Reports are private and are not sent to the reported player. Moderation records are retained while needed to investigate abuse or protect the service, then deleted or de-identified.

The game obtains service time to schedule seasons. It does not need your precise location or birth date. Current builds use restricted, non-personalised advertising because age-aware advertising treatment has not yet been verified. Advertising networks still process the technical ad data described above.

You can access this policy and request account/data deletion from Settings > Help, privacy & safety. Include the player ID displayed there so we can identify the correct account. A deletion request covers cloud progress, chosen names, leaderboard entries and published rooms. Some completed duel records may need to be anonymised rather than removed to preserve other players' results. Any retention required for purchases or abuse prevention is explained when we respond.

Community rules: Wall Runner community rules.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 plays the game, email us and we will delete its data.
